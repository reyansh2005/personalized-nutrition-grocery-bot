RECIPE: High-Protein Chicken and Rice
Ingredients: chicken breast, brown rice, spinach, olive oil, garlic.
Instructions: Cook rice. Pan-sear chicken in olive oil until fully cooked. Serve with spinach.
Nutrition estimate per serving: 520 calories, 42 g protein, 48 g carbohydrates, 16 g fat.
Allergy note: Use olive oil instead of butter for a dairy-free meal.

RECIPE: Budget Bean and Egg Bowl
Ingredients: canned beans, eggs, brown rice, spinach, olive oil.
Instructions: Warm beans, cook rice and eggs, then combine with spinach.
Nutrition estimate per serving: 460 calories, 27 g protein, 55 g carbohydrates, 14 g fat.
Allergy note: Naturally dairy-free; check packaged ingredients.

RECIPE: Indian Chickpea and Spinach Curry
Ingredients: chickpeas, spinach, onion, tomato, garlic, ginger, cumin, turmeric, garam masala, brown rice.
Instructions: Saute onion, garlic, and ginger. Add spices, tomato, chickpeas, and a little water. Simmer and fold in spinach. Serve with rice.
Nutrition estimate per serving: 490 calories, 20 g protein, 78 g carbohydrates, 11 g fat, 15 g fiber.
Allergy note: Naturally dairy-free when prepared without butter, cream, or yogurt.

RECIPE: Indian Masoor Dal with Roti
Ingredients: red lentils, whole-wheat roti, onion, tomato, garlic, cumin, turmeric, coriander, spinach.
Instructions: Simmer lentils with turmeric until soft. Temper cumin, onion, garlic, and tomato in oil, then mix into the dal. Serve with roti and spinach.
Nutrition estimate per serving: 450 calories, 23 g protein, 72 g carbohydrates, 9 g fat, 17 g fiber.
Allergy note: Naturally vegetarian and dairy-free; use oil instead of ghee.

RECIPE: Vegetable Moong Dal Khichdi

RECIPE: Rajma Rice Bowl

RECIPE: Besan Vegetable Chilla
Ingredients: chickpea flour, onion, tomato, spinach, green chili, cumin, water, olive oil.

RECIPE: Tofu Tikka Masala
Ingredients: firm tofu, tomato, onion, garlic, ginger, bell pepper, turmeric, garam masala, coconut milk.
Instructions: Bake or pan-sear tofu. Cook onion, spices, tomato, and coconut milk into a sauce, then add tofu and bell pepper.
Nutrition estimate per serving: 430 calories, 27 g protein, 32 g carbohydrates, 22 g fat.
Allergy note: Vegan and dairy-free; use certified gluten-free ingredients if required.

RECIPE: Peanut Vegetable Poha
Ingredients: flattened rice, peanuts, onion, peas, carrot, mustard seeds, turmeric, lemon.
Instructions: Rinse flattened rice. Temper mustard seeds, cook onion and vegetables, then fold in the flattened rice, peanuts, turmeric, and lemon.
Nutrition estimate per serving: 390 calories, 12 g protein, 61 g carbohydrates, 12 g fat.
Allergy note: Dairy-free; omit peanuts for a peanut allergy and use roasted chickpeas instead.

Allergy note: Dairy-free when prepared without cream or butter.

Allergy note: Dairy-free; use a nut-free seed butter when required.
